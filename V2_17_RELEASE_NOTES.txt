V2.17 - IFRAME NO-IMAGE GUARD

Fixes blank iframe cards caused by stale/PDF-only products that have no usable
live image and no previously cached known-good image.

Rules:
- ACTIVE with live image URL -> include.
- ACTIVE with known-good cached image -> include.
- SOLD OUT with known-good cached image -> include with SOLD OUT treatment.
- SOLD OUT/PDF-only item with no known-good image -> omit.
- Never invent a blank product card.

Christmas SKU 90343 is the motivating example:
it exists in the PDF, but the live Christmas category does not contain it and
there is no usable cached image, so the iframe now omits it just as the paper
booklet does.

This does not switch Christmas to scrape mode. That decision remains governed
by the daily PDF-vs-live audit.
