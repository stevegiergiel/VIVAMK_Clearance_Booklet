V2.19 - VISIBLE CONCISE DESCRIPTIONS ON PAPER BOOKLETS

Observed issue
--------------
The current six-products-per-page paper layout effectively leaves no visible
description area, so printed cards show only the product name and pricing.

Fix
---
- Slightly reduces the paper product-image area.
- Reserves a real description zone on every product card.
- Uses a concise summary derived from the fuller online product description.
- Normally keeps the first sentence, adding a second only when it remains short.
- Caps the summary at about 165 characters.
- Allows up to 3 compact print lines.
- SKU and price block remain protected and unchanged.
- Online iframe descriptions are unchanged.

This applies to all paper clearance booklets using the generic engine.
